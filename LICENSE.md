# Continuous Research — Open for Contribution

This repository is a **public knowledge base**, maintained by Bosser Oy and intentionally open for use and contribution by the broader community of agentic industry leaders.

## What You Can Do

- **Read and quote** any finding, synthesis, pattern, or observation here — with attribution.
- **Fork and redistribute** the research corpus, in whole or in part, provided the evidence standards documented in [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md) and [`research-rules.md`](research-rules.md) are respected and third-party sources cited inside remain credited to their original authors.
- **Contribute findings, user signals, and observations** back into the research base. Research runs continuously; practitioner contributions sharpen it.

## Contribution Terms

By contributing to this repository — via pull request, direct push (for trusted research agents and maintainers), or any other means — you grant **Bosser Oy and the community** a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use, adapt, redistribute, and build upon your contribution as part of the public research base. You retain authorship attribution where applicable.

Contributions must honour the research guidelines in [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md) and [`research-rules.md`](research-rules.md) — the evidence ladder, source-type classification, freshness rule, obfuscation rule for user signals, and the three admission gates per finding. Contributions that ignore those rules will be declined or edited to fit.
